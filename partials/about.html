I am currently a Frontend Engineer on the Dashboard team at Revelio Labs, where I work on developing and improving interactive data analytics dashboards. I recently completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC), where I was a graduate researcher at the Electronic Visualization Laboratory (EVL), advised by Dr. G. Elisabeta Marai.

My research interests lie in Data Visualization, Visual Analytics, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and Scientific Visualization. My doctoral research focused on developing interactive visualization techniques and systems for exploring, analyzing, and comparing complex scientific data, with applications in neurosurgery, oncology, computational fluid dynamics, and genomics.

Previously, I worked as a research intern at Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) and the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL).
