I design and build interactive visual analytics systems that help scientists and clinicians make sense of complex, high-dimensional data. My research — conducted at UIC's Electronic Visualization Laboratory — sits at the intersection of data visualization, human-computer interaction, and biomedical computing. I have contributed to tools that support oncologists in radiotherapy risk prediction, neurosurgeons in epilepsy pattern analysis, and computational scientists at Los Alamos National Laboratory and National Renewable Energy Laboratory in exploring large-scale 3D genome structures. My work has been published at IEEE VIS, IEEE EMBC, and the European Journal of Cancer, and featured in IEEE Computing Edge Magazine.
