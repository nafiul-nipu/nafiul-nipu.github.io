I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at UIC's Electronic Visualization Laboratory, advised by Dr. G. Elisabeta Marai. I design and build interactive visual analytics systems that help scientists and clinicians make sense of complex, high-dimensional data, at the intersection of data visualization, AI-driven, human-centered data science, advanced data analytics, and scalable machine learning.

My work has supported oncologists in radiotherapy risk prediction, neurosurgeons in epilepsy pattern analysis, computational fluid dynamics researchers in visualizing high-performance computing simulations of aircraft contrail formation, and computational scientists at Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) and National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) in exploring large-scale 3D genome structures. I am also developing AI-assisted systems for automated, provenance-aware scientific workflow generation. My research has been published at IEEE VIS, IEEE EMBC, Computer Graphics Forum, and the European Journal of Cancer, and featured in IEEE Computing Edge Magazine.
